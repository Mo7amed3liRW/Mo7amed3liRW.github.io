Tower crane model

Rev C issued

Commissioned scale model for a construction-equipment company (client name withheld).

What was designed

A lattice-mast tower crane with a trussed jib, a counter-jib carrying stacked counterweight plates, pendant ties from the apex, a hoist rope to the hook block and ballast pads around the mast foot.

The model went through three revisions. The last one carries a full dimensioned drawing set with a 29-item assembly sheet.

RoleDesign and drawings
ToolsSolidWorks
DeliveredAssembly, drawings, renders

Revisions

Drawing set

Site photos

Balance screening Preliminary estimate

Read from the drawing geometry, with no rated load assigned. Hook reach is about 800 drawing units and the counterweight arm about 200, a ratio of 4 to 1.

  • To balance a hook load Q at full reach, the counterweight must weigh about 4 × Q, plus whatever the jib's own weight adds.
  • The four ballast pads at the mast foot carry the remaining overturning moment to the ground.
  • The ratio does not depend on the drawing scale, so it holds for a scale model and for a full-size version.

A4

Wall-mounted jib crane, 250 kg

Hand calc + FEA

A twin-truss cantilever jib bolted to a building wall, with a trolley hoist on its runway. Designed from a sketch, calculated by hand to a lifting-appliance method, then checked in SolidWorks Simulation.

250 kgrated hook load
1.5 mreach: three 0.5 m bays, 0.5 m deep
SHS 40×40×4S355J2H steel, K-braced web
FOS 8.35hand check, worst chord, limit 2
0.15 mmtip deflection in FEA, limit 2.5 mm (L/600)

Scope

  • Two parallel K-trusses joined by six cross members, pinned to the wall at the bottom chord and tied back at the top chord.
  • Trolley with wire-rope hoist running on two U-section rails under the bottom chord.
  • Loads per EN 13001-style practice: hoisting class HC2, dynamic factor 1.14, trolley and hoist dead load, self-weight at every node, 10% connection allowance.
  • Hoist drive sized: 406 W required, 0.55 kW 4-pole motor with 55:1 worm reduction selected.

Role: concept, CAD, hand calculations, FEA. Tools: SolidWorks, SolidWorks Simulation (beam mesh). The hoist and hook use catalogue geometry.

Concept to model

Hand calculation against FEA

Quantity (one truss)HandFEA
Wall reaction, horizontal5,520 N5,330 N
Wall reaction, vertical2,085 N1,980 N
Tip deflection0.134 mm0.148 mm
Largest member force5,520 N3,401 N
Peak stress42.5 MPa6.6 MPa

Reactions agree within 5%. The hand check is deliberately conservative: it puts the whole horizontal reaction through one chord and adds local runway bending from the trolley wheel load, which the node-loaded FEA does not include. Both are far below yield.

Load path

  • Tip node: (2,798 N amplified hook load + 294 N trolley) / 2 trusses + 49 N self-weight = 1,595 N.
  • Moment about the lower pin gives 5,520 N in the top link and the same in the runway chord.
  • Combined stress 9.9 MPa axial + 32.7 MPa bending = 42.5 MPa against 355 MPa yield.
  • FEA: 316 beam elements, 332 nodes, linear static, gravity plus 1,546 N nodal load per truss.

The FEA study uses SolidWorks' generic steel (250 MPa yield); the factor of safety above uses S355J2H properties.

Automated mushroom harvesting machine

Design and detection

A picking robot for Dutch mushroom racks, with a fin-ray gripper chosen to lift soft caps without bruising them, and a YOLOv5 detector to find the mushrooms.

1 per 2 spick rate the design is sized for
3 fingersfin-ray gripper on linkages
4 dayssolo design time
11hand-labelled images
0.51mAP@0.5, YOLOv5m, 3 validation images

Scope

  • Cartesian picking robot on a wheeled frame, built to travel between Dutch growing racks without changing the rack.
  • Two uprights roll along the long rails, a cross beam spans them, and a trolley carries the gripper along it.
  • Three-finger gripper: parallelogram linkages carry ribbed fin-ray fingers that bend around the cap instead of pinching it.
  • Picking motions reduced to pull, twist and tilt, to keep the degrees of freedom low.
  • Stereo camera and a YOLOv5 detector to build the picking queue.

Role: sole engineer. Mechanical design in SolidWorks, picking strategy, detector training. Done during the JoVision traineeship, on request, in four working days. Electronics and control software were not built in that time.
